About MP Benti

Founded by Paolo Benti, MP Benti supplies restaurants and businesses across Sydney with authentic Italian dairy, smallgoods and pantry staples.

With deep relationships across local producers and importers, Paolo curates a compact but reliable catalogue focused on quality and consistency.

We remain a family-run wholesaler built on trust, service and a passion for great food.
